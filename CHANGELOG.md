# FT's Geology: Feel the Nature 1.2.0

The country's small details, its mornings, and the fire lookouts on its summits.

- **Stones at the water's edge**: in the shallows of FT's Geology's rivers, a block or two from the bank, of the rock
  FT's Geology lays there; some mossy, some with their backs out of the water. Fewer round a lake.
- **Old snow on the north slopes**: patches lying on into the summer on the shaded slopes of the alpine meadows, the
  tree line and the scree, deepest under a step in the slope.
- **Beside a fallen log** in a mossy wood, the ground rotting to podzol, and mushrooms in it.
- **The spray at a waterfall's foot**: mossy rock, moss and ferns, and no snow, round where each of FT's Geology's falls
  lands, out over its banks.
- **Morning mist**: on a calm, clear, cool morning, mist rising off still water, thickest just after sunrise and in
  autumn; not in a wind, in rain, in the heat, or where FT's Geology's own fog already lies.
- **Motes in the sun**: dust glinting in the shafts of light through a wood's crowns on a still, dry day.
- **Snow off the crowns**: a gust shakes the snow off the trees that hold it.
- **Rain on the windows**: indoors in the rain, drops running down the glass, clinging and sliding, more the harder it
  rains.
- **New rain recordings**: a softer light rain on a tree canopy, a mountain rain for the steady one, and three
  downpours' beginnings to come by turns; the umbrella closes with a sound of its own, and its canvas snaps as it
  blows inside out. Frogs a little quieter.
- **Fire lookouts** on prominent summits of hill and mountain country, no two nearer than 660 blocks, as
  the real ones are built: a glazed cab twenty blocks up on four splayed timber legs braced in X, a switchback stair
  up the middle walked without a jump and lit by lanterns under its landings, coming out onto a walkway carried on
  brackets (the cab is entered by its door from the walkway, so nothing climbs straight into the room), a hipped roof
  with a little glazed lantern-room rising out of it. Kept as a weather station with FT's Geology's instruments (the terminal at a desk with the
  barometer and hygrometer hung on the wall over it, the rain gauge out on the walkway, the thermometer hung in the
  shade on the cab's north corner post, the anemometer on the lantern-room's peak, a lightning rod, all of them read
  by the terminal) and lived in: a bed, a stove and its chimney, lanterns, the fire finder in the middle of the
  floor, barrels of stores and a visitors' book, the keeper's log open on a lectern by the door (in the
  reader's own language, the rescue frequency on its first page). The legs stand into the ground or on stone footings,
  mossy with the years; in a wet country, vines up them. Within sight of Everest, K2 or the Matterhorn a lookout faces
  its summit, with a board by the door naming it. With FT's Geology's API 5 its earthquakes leave a lookout and
  its radio mast alone (the shaking still felt). `/ftn locate lookout` finds the nearest.
- **A trail** down from each lookout's stair, winding to keep a walker's slope, to the river or the valley's floor; a
  board at its foot pointing up to the lookout. On a few trees along it, a rescue board: the rescue frequency, and
  which way and how far the lookout lies.
- **With the Simple Radio ham fork**: each lookout a radio station too, on the rescue frequency, 145.500 MHz: a
  station in the cab with a desk microphone beside it, linked to an antenna on a mast in an enclosure of iron bars
  beside the tower, its battery charged when the tower was found but running down within minutes, as an abandoned
  station's would. At the foot of a leg a coal generator stands cold, its wire laid up the leg and across the floor
  to the station: a few coal, and the radio is lit again.
- **With Advanced Chimneys**, the stoves' chimneys are its own; **with Pollution of the Realms**, no fire is left
  burning on them.
- **Flag trees** at the tree line and on the high pastures: lone spruces with their branches streaming to leeward and
  their tops bent over, every one on a mountainside pointing the way FT's Geology's prevailing wind blows.
- **Cairns** on the bare tops of the alpine meadows and the scree, of the rock under them.
- **Mountain shelters** here and there on the high pastures: a dry-stone hut with a pitched roof, a stove and a bunk,
  stores in a barrel, firewood stacked under the eaves and a rain butt by the door. On its wall, where a fire lookout
  stands within a couple of kilometres, a map to it: used, it unfolds into a map of the country round the lookout,
  the lookout marked.
- **Benches** where the ground falls away before a view.
- **Bird hides** on the shores of FT's Geology's lakes, a slit to watch the water through.
- **With Naturalist**: deer, bears, boars, finches, butterflies and sparrows in FT's Geology's own forests and meadows,
  which Naturalist did not know for forest.

The stones' rock, the falls, the flag trees, the lookouts' faster siting and their named mountains want FT's Geology
0.11.4 or newer (its API 4); with an older one the addon goes the old way, and grows no flag trees.

# FT's Geology: Feel the Nature 1.1.0

The country's creatures, heard where they would be.

- **Birds by day** in what grows round you: songbirds in broadleaf woods, a quieter chorus in the conifers, skylarks
  over open grass.
- **The dawn chorus**: from an hour before sunrise, strongest just after it, fullest in spring.
- **Crickets** on warm, dry nights; **frogs** by still fresh water in the evening (a light rain does not stop them);
  **bees** over flowers on dry days.
- **Now and then a single call** from somewhere: a hawk high over open country by day, an owl from a tree in a wood
  at night, a loon far out on a large lake in a cool country.
- Rain and gales quiet the birds, cold ground and winter quiet the insects and the frogs; with Serene Seasons each
  keeps its season.
- A `faunaVolume` setting in the client config (0 turns them off).
- Recordings from Freesound, all CC0 but the loon's (nicStage, CC BY 4.0); credited in `CREDITS-sounds.md`.

# FT's Geology: Feel the Nature 1.0.0

The first release. Forge 1.20.1 (47.2 or newer), with FT's Geology 0.11.0 or newer.

---

## The weather, heard where you stand

- **Rain as it falls on you**, by FT's Geology's regional weather: a drizzle, a steady rain and a downpour, each
  its own recording, one handing over to the next as the rain strengthens. A rain beginning after a dry spell is heard
  beginning; a long rain dying away is heard ending.
- **What it falls on**: leaves and conifer needles, open water, bare rock, the crown of a tree over you, a wooden,
  metal or glass roof, a window. Drips go on falling from the leaves after the rain has stopped.
- **Snow falls quietly.** No rain is heard where it snows. A drizzle at a storm's edge is a quiet patter, heard as soon
  as it is seen falling.
- **Wind** from a breeze to a gale, strongest on the heights, in the leaves, the pines and the grass round you. It
  swells and lulls with FT's Geology's gusts, the same gusts on every client. In a storm the trees creak now and then.
- **Water**: a brook running over the ground, FT's Geology's rivers where they run (a river stilled behind a dam
  is as quiet as a pond), and falling water.
- **Shelter**: underground the weather is not heard; through a doorway, a little of it.
- Vanilla's rain and FT's Geology's own rain recordings stand down while the addon plays the weather; with
  AmbientSounds installed, its rain and wind do too.

## The umbrella

- Sixteen colours: three wool of the colour over two iron nuggets and two sticks; dye it another colour at the
  crafting table.
- Open it to hear the rain drum on its cloth over your head. With Tough As Nails it keeps you dry.
- A strong gust (from about 14 m/s, the gusts of a Beaufort 6 wind) turns it inside out and shuts it; the lulls of a
  gale spare it, a breeze never turns it. Mend it with iron nuggets.

## The small life

- Fireflies over the grass on warm, dry nights; pollen over the flowers by day; leaves falling under broadleaf trees,
  more of them in the wind and in autumn (with Serene Seasons); loose snow blown off the ground in a strong wind.

## The country

- **FT's Geology's mountain belts clothed**: alpine meadow flowers, dwarf spruce at the tree line, mossy montane
  forest with fallen logs, ferns and lily of the valley, subalpine spruce, scree. Boulders are cut from the rock under
  them: gneiss where the ground is gneiss, basalt on basalt.
- **River banks**: tall grass, ferns and, where it is warm, reeds along FT's Geology's rivers and lakes, lily pads on
  a lake's shallow margin. Not along a river that has sunk underground.
- **Volcanic bombs** round the craters of FT's Geology's large volcanoes, thickest towards the summit.
- **Basalt sea coasts**: in Terralith's basalt cliff biome its field of spires is replaced by columns along the sea,
  upright in a cliff's face, leaning on its foot, and stepping down into the water.
- **Music** by the country: forest, meadow, peaks, snow, stony ground, taiga, the tree line.

## Works with

FT's Geology (required), Serene Seasons, Tough As Nails, AmbientSounds, Terralith. Everything but FT's Geology is
optional.
